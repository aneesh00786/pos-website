Complete Specifications

Everything Your Retail Checkout Needs

All 20 modules from our complete POS specification document — engineered to handle rush hours, stock control, and financial reconciliation.

1. Modern POS Billing System

Fast, tactile split-screen checkout. Create new sales, filter by category, sort A-Z, view real-time stock availability, and browse grid or list view.

  • Fast & simple cashier interface
  • USB/HID & Camera scanning
  • Responsive desktop, tablet & POS

2. Shopping Cart / Current Sale

Instant reactive cart ticket. Add products, use touch +/- quantity steppers or manual numeric keypad, apply item discounts, and clear cart in 1 click.

  • Zero page refresh recalculation
  • Subtotal, tax and discount lines
  • Live stock validation guards

3. Tax Management

Category-based automatic tax calculation. Supports tax-inclusive pricing, tax-exclusive pricing, zero-tax goods, and consistent currency rounding.

  • Tax snapshot saved with every sale
  • GST, VAT and multi-tax support
  • Clear breakdown on receipt

4. Customer CRM & Loyalty

Search customers by phone, name, or barcode. Create new customer profiles on the fly, assign to sale before completion, or process fast guest checkout.

  • View customer details & credit balance
  • Customer-specific discount eligibility
  • Complete purchase history link

5. Promotions & Discounts

View available promotional deals, verify promo code eligibility server-side, and apply automated percentage or fixed dollar discounts.

  • Bill-level & item-level promotions
  • Automatic savings display
  • Discount audit trail stored in ledger

6. Hold & Resume Sales

Customer forgot their wallet? Put the sale on hold with custom customer remarks, ring up the next customer in line, and resume anytime.

  • Multi-cart parking repository
  • Visual active held sales counter
  • Seamless resume and bill completion

7. Multi-Tender Payments

Accept Cash with quick tender buttons and automated change calculation. Native integration for Card, dynamic UPI QR code, and Pine Labs cloud terminals.

  • Pine Labs payment terminal bridge
  • Insufficient payment alert validation
  • Stripe & Card Reader integration

8. Bill Completion & Invoicing

Atomic server-side validation. Recalculates prices, verifies stock availability, generates sequential invoice numbers (e.g. POS-2026-XXXX), and saves snapshot.

  • Guaranteed price/tax snapshot
  • Atomic stock decrement
  • Instant receipt print trigger

9. Sales History & Returns

Comprehensive ledger of all completed sales. Search by transaction number, filter by date range or cashier, view receipt details, and reprint anytime.

  • Filter by date, cashier, or tender
  • Product-level breakdown
  • Void / return transaction workflows

10. Receipt Management

Dedicated ESC/POS formatting for 58mm mobile printers and 80mm commercial thermal counter printers with store header, tax summary, and auto-cut trigger.

  • 58mm & 80mm paper roll formats
  • USB, LAN & Bluetooth printer drivers
  • Custom logo & footer thank-you note

11. Barcode Scanning

Global keystroke listener intercepts USB/HID laser scanner guns instantly without focusing on input fields. Plus mobile camera viewfinder with audio beep confirmation.

  • Plug-and-play USB scanner capture
  • Tablet/phone camera barcode detector
  • Direct add-to-cart in milliseconds

12. Inventory Integration

Live inventory synchronization. Checks available inventory on scan, blocks sale of out-of-stock items, and automatically updates quantities upon checkout.

  • Automatic stock decrement
  • Low-stock threshold warnings
  • Stock movements & quick adjust

13. 100% Offline POS Mode

Keep ringing up sales even when the internet goes down. Uses IndexedDB local catalog caching and queues pending transactions, syncing automatically when back online.

  • Zero downtime during internet outages
  • Automatic reconciliation queue
  • Duplicate transaction protection

14. Staff PIN Security

4-digit cashier PIN login, instant register lock button, session timeout protection, and role-based access to settings and refund actions.

  • Fast touchscreen PIN keypad
  • Lock register with 1 click
  • Audit logs for every cashier action

15 & 16. Shift & Cash Drawer Z-Reports

Declare opening cash floats before ringing sales. Track all cash in/out movements, and close shifts with physical denomination counting and printable Z-reports.

  • Opening cash float verification
  • Expected cash vs counted discrepancy
  • Thermal Z-Report printout

17 & 18. Real-Time POS Dashboard

Monitor today's total revenue, transaction counts, cash vs card vs UPI breakdowns, average ticket size, active shift drawer, and low-stock alerts.

  • Live KPI statistics & payment splits
  • Responsive on desktop, tablet, and phone
  • Recent sales stream

19 & 20. Sales Reports & Full Navigation

Full suite of business reports: daily sales, date-range, by cashier, by category, and by payment method. Complete Section 20 navigation menu.

  • Export reports for accounting
  • Tax & shift reconciliation audits
  • Full 11-module POS system
