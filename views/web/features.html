MODULE 11 & 13

Instant Barcode Scanning & Zero-Downtime Offline Mode

Cashiers never have to touch a mouse or click a search field. Pointing a handheld or omni-directional USB barcode gun automatically adds products to the active cart in milliseconds with an audible confirmation tone.

If your broadband internet drops or Wi-Fi flickers, the terminal continues processing sales uninterrupted using its local offline cache, queuing transactions to prevent lost revenue.
