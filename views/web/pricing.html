FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when the 14-day free trial ends?

At the end of your 14-day trial, you can choose to enter payment details to continue using your license keys. If you decide not to continue, your account simply pauses without surprise charges.

Can I run ThriveWoo POS without an internet connection?

Yes! All plans include 100% offline vault mode. Cashiers can continue barcode scanning, ringing up cart items, and printing receipts. When internet restores, transactions synchronize automatically.

Do I need to purchase specific hardware?

No. ThriveWoo POS runs on standard PCs, Macs, iPads, and Android tablets. It supports standard USB/HID barcode scanners and any standard 58mm or 80mm ESC/POS thermal printer.
