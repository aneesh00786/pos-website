5-Minute Quickstart Guide

Getting started with ThriveWoo POS takes less than 5 minutes on any device running a modern browser:

  1. Obtain a License Key: Sign up for a 14-day free trial on our Pricing Page or generate a key inside your Customer Portal.
  2. Launch the Terminal: Navigate to /pos/sale on your store's computer or tablet.
  3. Sign In / Unlock: Enter your 4-digit cashier PIN (default demo PIN: 1234 or 0000).
  4. Declare Shift Opening Float: Enter the initial cash in the drawer (e.g. $150.00) to open the register.
  5. Scan and Ring Up: Use a USB barcode gun or click items on the screen to add them to the ticket and tender payment.

ESC/POS Thermal Printer Configuration

ThriveWoo POS generates pixel-perfect receipts formatted for standard 80mm and 58mm thermal rolls.

Pro Tip: For USB and network receipt printers, set your default browser print margins to "None" and disable "Headers and footers" for seamless thermal cuts.

To test your printer, navigate to Settings → Receipt Printer in the POS terminal and click "Send Test Print".

USB & Camera Barcode Scanner Setup

ThriveWoo POS features a global keystroke listener that intercepts barcode input anywhere on the screen:

  • USB/HID Scanners: Simply plug the USB cable into your terminal. No configuration or drivers needed. The scanner sends rapid keystrokes with an ENTER terminator which the POS immediately captures.
  • Camera Scanner: Click the camera icon in the search bar on tablets or laptops to open the live video viewfinder. It utilizes the hardware-accelerated BarcodeDetector API with Web Audio beep feedback.
